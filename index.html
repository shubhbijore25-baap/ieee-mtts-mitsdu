About

Powering the technology behind wireless life

From 5G and satellites to radar and IoT, microwave engineering connects the world. Our chapter helps students at MITS-DU explore it hands-on.

📡

What is IEEE MTT-S?

A global society of IEEE advancing RF, microwave, millimeter-wave and terahertz technologies through research, publications and conferences.

🎓

Our Chapter

A student-run chapter at MITS-DU Gwalior offering workshops, expert talks, projects and competitions for curious minds from every branch.

🚀

Our Mission

To build practical skills, connect students with the IEEE network and prepare them for careers and research in wireless technologies.
